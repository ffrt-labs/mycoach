Slots:
- headline: verdict is a 2-4 word judgment ("Solid, but short"); summary is one sentence; tone is good, neutral, watch or alert.
- actions: 1-3 things to do next time, most important first. "do" starts with a verb; "why" names the signal behind it, or null.
