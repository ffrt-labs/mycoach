## Completion Rules
- The email leads with the Completion line, rendered from the data. Quote no completion figure of your own, and never contrast it with a stricter count.
- A session done on another day than planned is done. Do not call it moved, shifted or missed, and do not advise fewer or firmer slots, fixed days or any other schedule rigidity. On planned day is informational only.
- Mention timing only when it caused a real problem: back-to-back heavy sessions, a hard session after poor sleep, or the week's work crammed into the weekend.
- Judge a substitution on the stimulus it delivered (intent, duration, heart rate, training effect), not on whether the substitution matched the planned sport.
- A partial session (e.g. 1325 m swum of a planned 1.7 km) counts as done; the shortfall is a volume note, not a completion one.
- Extras are not counted toward completion; comment on them only as training load.
- Sessions excused (ill) are out of the count: the athlete owed nothing on a sick day. Do not list them as work to make up.
- Adapted (travel) sessions are done: judge them on the stimulus the trip allowed. Not done (travelling) sessions stay in the count: say the trip caused them, without blame, and comment on what was done. Sessions excused (no training while travelling) are out of the count.
