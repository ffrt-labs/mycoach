MyCoach · Email system v2

Read in 10 seconds. Act on it.

The LLM fills typed slots with length budgets; code renders every number. Long analysis moves to the dashboard behind “Full analysis →”.

Principles
01
Verdict and actions first
Every email opens with a verdict, one sentence, and at most three actions. Everything below is evidence.
02
Numbers come from code
Pace, zones, HRV, totals and trends are rendered from the DB (as HRV/steps/body already are). The LLM writes judgments, never metrics.
03
Every LLM string has a slot and a budget
No free paragraphs in the email. Fields have max lengths and list caps; the long write-up goes to analysis_md for the dashboard.
04
Conditions are rules, not prose
“If HRV < 65 or RHR > 45 → Z2 only” renders as a when/then row you can scan in the morning.
05
System issues are not coaching
Missing data, failed plans and routine typos render as a separate notice with a fix link, never inside advice.
06
Week-scoped, glossary words only (PR #223)
Completion is per Mon–Sun week (ADR 0005). Statuses, intents and episodes are code-rendered with CONTEXT.md labels — never “missed” or “moved”.
